🔥 Virtual Food Energy Lab

Year 8 Science • Measuring the energy released when food burns
BEFORE YOU START

Aim

To investigate how much energy is released when a small piece of food is burned and transferred to water.

Key idea: When food burns, chemical energy is released. In this virtual experiment, some of that energy is transferred to the water, causing its temperature to increase.

Hypothesis

Which food do you predict will release the most energy?

PART 5

Think like a scientist

Our experiment measures all of the chemical energy in the food. Do you agree?

Think about: Not all of the heat produced by burning food reaches the water. Some energy can be transferred to the surroundings, the apparatus and the air, and some food may not burn completely.
